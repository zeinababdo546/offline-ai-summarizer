Managing your time effectively is essential for achieving personal and professional success without experiencing burnout. One popular technique is the Pomodoro Method, where you work with full focus for 25 minutes, followed by a 5-minute break. This keeps your brain fresh and prevents mental fatigue throughout the day.

Another critical habit is prioritizing your daily tasks using the Eisenhower Matrix. By categorizing activities into urgent vs. important, you can focus on high-impact work while eliminating or delegating low-priority distractions.

Getting enough sleep and setting clear boundaries between study, work, and personal life are essential for long-term productivity. Taking short walk breaks and staying hydrated also significantly improves focus and cognitive performance over time.
